## ideas

Make every concept achievable as a short film: a small cast, few locations and one clear dramatic idea.

## originality_review

A short film turns on one idea: look hardest at its central idea, its twist and its ending.

## originality_compare

A short film turns on one idea: a shared central idea, twist or ending matters more than shared details.

## engagement_scene

A short film has no time to spare: each scene should change something, though a short quiet moment can earn its place.

## engagement_summary

A short film turns on one idea: judge how fast the opening sets it up and whether the ending pays it off.
