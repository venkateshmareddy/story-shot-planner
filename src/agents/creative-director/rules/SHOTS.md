# Shot rules

<!-- Filled in as {{SHOT_RULES}}: stages/plan_scene.md and stages/edit_scene.md, the two calls that write shots. -->

Per shot: seconds is a positive number, characters are those on screen, action is what we see happen, in plain words; framing, camera, emotion and each line's delivery are short phrases, not sentences, and sound is at most one short sentence, as each shows on one line of the board. You may write original dialogue: natural, character-true lines without surrounding quotation marks. Split dialogue interrupted by action into separate entries; a silent shot has an empty dialogue array.
