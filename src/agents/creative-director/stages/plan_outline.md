This call writes the film's outline; separate calls then write each scene's shots from it. Input sections: STORY (working title or story), ANSWERS (the filmmaker's interview answers), DIRECTIONS (their extra directions after the interview), MEMORY — what we know, REMOVED by the filmmaker, OPEN QUESTIONS and COLLECTED (collected creative details not in MEMORY). {{MEMORY_RULE}} Treat the answers, directions, collected details and every From the filmmaker or Approved fact as binding: use them exactly as given, including edited ones, and never contradict them. AI suggestions and collected details marked (AI suggestion) are suggestions you may refine. REMOVED lists details the filmmaker deleted: never use them. Where details are missing, make sensible choices and list the key assumptions in reviewNotes, one short sentence each. Start each reviewNotes entry with "Assumption:" (a choice made for a missing detail) or "Suggestion:" (a recommendation, gap or contradiction for the filmmaker to consider). Keep all material story beats in order and make the stated intent actionable. Return the title, a one-sentence logline, the intent in one or two sentences, every character (characters: role in a few words; look, personality and voice one or two short sentences each) and place (locations: description in one or two short sentences) the film needs, and the scenes in story order: at least 1 and at most {{MAX_PLAN_SCENES}}, each at most 75 seconds (split a longer stretch into consecutive scenes). Per scene: a short title, a summary of what happens in order (with key lines the story gives), its location and characters (names written exactly as in locations and characters) and its planned seconds; together the scenes fill the film's intended length. {{SUGGESTED_CONCEPT_RULE}} Add one Suggestion: note naming the key parts taken from it that the filmmaker has not yet confirmed, so they can confirm or change them.
