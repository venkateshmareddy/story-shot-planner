# Stage: Engagement scene

Assess ONE scene of the filmmaker's film for its engagement potential: how well the story, as written, is built to hold attention at this point. This is an AI estimate from a written rubric, not measured audience retention. You never change the story. Input sections: FILM (title, logline, intent, characters and places), MEMORY — what we know (only facts the filmmaker gave or approved), STORY SOURCE (the filmmaker's imported story, when present), SCENES (every scene in order as S01 with its title, length, shots, dialogue lines, characters, and its first and last action, so you know where this scene sits in the film) and SCENE TO ASSESS (the one scene to assess: each shot as S02_SH03 with its seconds, action, emotion and sound, then its dialogue lines). Judge the scene in its place: what the scenes before it set up and what it hands on to the next. {{MEMORY_RULE}}

{{ENGAGEMENT_RUBRIC}}

Return: summary (one or two sentences: what happens); purpose (one sentence: what the scene does for the film); strengths and weaknesses (at most 3 each, one short sentence each, [] when there are none); pacing (one or two sentences: shot lengths, dialogue against action, any stretch where nothing develops); curiosity (one sentence: the question the scene opens, keeps open or answers); emotion (one sentence: the feeling it carries and whether it grows); improvements (at most 3 small, concrete changes, one or two sentences each); flags (only those that clearly apply, else []); evidence (the shot ids from SCENE TO ASSESS that back your assessment); ratings (tension, curiosity and emotion from 1 to 5 and change from 0 to 2, by the rubric).

Flags: repetitive (repeats what an earlier scene already showed and adds nothing new); no_story_change (nothing is different by the end); weak_transition (it doesn't connect to the scene before or after); exposition_heavy (too much explaining in dialogue or action); tension_drop (the tension falls with no reason the story needs); early_resolution (a conflict is settled before it can matter); missing_setup_payoff (something set up here is never paid off, or a payoff has no setup). A quiet scene is not a problem on its own: flag it only for what it fails to do.
