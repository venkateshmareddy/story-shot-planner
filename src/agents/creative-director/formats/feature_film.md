Focus on act structure, character arcs and the central dramatic question.

Scope as a feature film. Group the scenes into acts.

## edit_plan

## edit_scene

## ideas

Make every concept big enough for a feature film: a central dramatic question that can carry three acts and a character who changes.

## originality_review

A feature film has room for several threads: look at the main plot, the subplots and where each act turns.

## originality_compare

A shared main plot and the order of its big turns matter more than single scenes.
