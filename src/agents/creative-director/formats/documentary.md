## ideas

Make every concept a documentary idea: a real kind of subject, place or event that could be researched and filmed, with mainCharacter as the central subject. Never invent facts about real, named people.

## originality_review

Real events, people and facts belong to no one: look only at how the film tells them, such as its structure, framing, distinctive sequences and narration.

## originality_compare

A shared real subject or event is never a finding: compare only how the stories are told.
