# Format: Music video

## draft

Shape it as a visual interpretation driven by the track's mood and lyrics.

## interview

Focus on the song's structure, visual motifs and how image meets rhythm.

## plan_outline

Scope as a music video that follows the track's sections.

## plan_scene

Cut to the beat and favor short, rhythmic shots.

## edit_plan

Keep the plan scoped as a music video synced to its track.

## edit_scene

Cut to the beat and favor short, rhythmic shots.

## ideas

Make every concept work as a music video: a visual idea that follows a song's mood and rhythm, with little or no dialogue.

## originality_review

The song and its lyrics belong to the song: look at the visual idea, the story told on screen and any distinctive sequence.

## originality_compare

Compare the visual idea and the story told on screen, not the song.
