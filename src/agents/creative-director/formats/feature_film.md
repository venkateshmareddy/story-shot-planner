# Format: Feature film

<!-- One short line per stage, added after the stage text. Section names are the stage ids in src/lib/server/prompts.ts. -->

## draft

Shape it as a feature with a clear three-act arc and strong through-line.

## interview

Focus on act structure, character arcs and the central dramatic question.

## plan_outline

Scope as a feature film. Group the scenes into acts.

## plan_scene

Vary shot length to fit the scene.

## edit_plan

Keep the plan scoped as a feature film across its acts.

## edit_scene

Vary shot length to fit the scene.

## ideas

Make every concept big enough for a feature film: a central dramatic question that can carry three acts and a character who changes.

## originality_review

A feature film has room for several threads: look at the main plot, the subplots and where each act turns.

## originality_compare

A shared main plot and the order of its big turns matter more than single scenes.

## engagement_scene

A feature has room for quieter scenes between turns: judge each scene by what it adds to its act.

## engagement_summary

Look at the shape across the acts: a clear hook, pressure that rises, a turn in the middle and an earned climax.
