# Format: Advertisement

<!-- One short line per stage, added after the stage text. Section names are the stage ids in src/lib/server/prompts.ts. -->

## draft

Shape it around one persuasive message and a single memorable idea.

## interview

Focus on the audience, the core message, the call to action and the brand tone.

## plan_outline

Scope as an advertisement. Keep it tight, building to the message.

## plan_scene

Keep shots short and fast, building to the message.

## edit_plan

Keep the plan scoped as a short, persuasive advertisement.

## edit_scene

Keep shots short and fast, building to the message.

## ideas

Make every concept work as an advertisement: one clear message carried by a short, memorable story.

## originality_review

An advertisement is remembered by its central gag, twist and tagline: look hardest at those.

## originality_compare

A shared central gag, twist or tagline matters most.

## engagement_scene

An advertisement is very short: judge each moment by how quickly it makes its point.

## engagement_summary

Judge the hook in the first seconds and the ending by how clearly it lands the message.
